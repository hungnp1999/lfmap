`web.fetch` reads `gzip` and `deflate` pages (#218 WEB-4, #289): the fetcher sends `Accept-Encoding: gzip, deflate` and decompresses with `zlib` in bounded steps; `max_bytes` counts decompressed output and decoding stops at the cap (`truncated`), wire bytes are bounded by cap + 64 KiB, a corrupt or cut stream is `bad_response`, and `br`, `zstd`, stacked or unknown `Content-Encoding` and any `Transfer-Encoding` other than `chunked` stay refused with a message that names the header and value. No new dependency.
