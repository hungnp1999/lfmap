Data classes in the approval gate (#197 DC-2, ADR-025): with `[data.classes]` in `config.toml`, a per-call `ClassGuard` (`lfmap_core.data_guard`) sees every path the Permission Manager resolves. Reads under a `secret` entry are decided at T1 and always audited, creates under `secret` at T2 (asked, or a proposal for jobs), once per call per entry; `ctx.require_approval("overwrite", paths=...)` drops to T1 when every declared and written path is `scratch`. T3 is never lowered, and an error in the adjustment keeps the action tier and the `secret` floors. New `ctx.data_label` and `ctx.mark_data(path)`; audit lines about a classified path gain `data_class` and `action_tier`. `markitdown.export` declares its output folder. Without `[data.classes]` nothing changes.
