Event-triggered flows now get one bus subscription each, `flows-<id>`, with the flow's own pattern, instead of one shared `flows` subscription with a covering pattern. Changing or removing a flow only affects that flow's subscription and queued deliveries; subscriptions of flows that no longer exist are removed on attach, and `*.*.*`-style patterns no longer pull `flows.run.*` events into the handler. `EventBus` gains `subscriptions()` (name to pattern) in the Protocol, `InProcessBus` and `SqliteBus`. One-time cost: the legacy `flows` subscription is removed on the first attach and its queued deliveries are dropped. `flows_run.py` is unchanged.
